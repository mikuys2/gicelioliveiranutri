# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS + vanilla JS, single page (`index.html`), self-hosted fonts and images under `fonts/` and `img/`. Same convention as the other client redesigns in `C:\Clientes\` (brief-pinned, not asked separately).

## Users

Mostly women in Feira de Santana and Salvador (BA) who want to lose weight, are tired of diets that fail or of spending on "milagrosas" formulas, and are deciding whether to book a nutritionist. They arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or Google Maps, and act through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Clínica de Nutrição Avançada Dra. Giceli Oliveira (CRN5 8265). Success = the visitor taps a WhatsApp CTA to schedule a consultation.

## Positioning

Reeducação nutricional sem proibições e sem passar fome: individual plan built around the patient's preferences, routine and genetic profile; long first consultation (about 1h30) with physical assessment, semiology, diet delivered in 8 days, physical report and the nutri's app with direct chat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (site): Emagrecimento, Saúde da Mulher, Esteatose Hepática, Doenças Metabólicas, Nutrição e Saúde Gastrointestinal, Exame de Bioimpedância Elétrica (30 min), Testes Nutrigenéticos. All 1h30 except bioimpedância. Prices: "Consulte valores".
- Does not accept health plans; issues receipt for reimbursement.
- Two units (Google Maps, 2026): Feira de Santana (Av. Getúlio Vargas, 792, Edf. Ícone Tower, sala 607) and Salvador (CEO Salvador Shopping, Torre Londres, 4º andar, sala 405).
- WhatsApp: both (71) 98673-1801 (official whats.link) and (75) 99169-7851 (Google listings). User asked for both. Unit mapping by DDD is an inference.
- No offer/plans section: the user removed it (2026-10-11). Prices remain "Consulte valores".
- Hours follow Google Maps (user decision): Feira de Santana 24 horas todos os dias; Salvador todos os dias 7h às 22h.

## Brand Commitments

Logo (gold DNA/pineapple helix + "Clínica de Nutrição Avançada / Dra. Giceli Oliveira"), forest green #073419 + gold, her own photos. Visitor must recognize it as her page. Voice: warm, first person, "Vem comigo".

## Evidence on Hand

- Google: 5,0 with 123 reviews (Feira) + 5,0 with 26 (Salvador). Excerpts used with first name + initial.
- Prêmio Top of Mind Brazil 2019/20 (INBRAP), Melhor Nutricionista de Feira de Santana.
- Certificado de Excelência Doctoralia 2019.
- Press/community: TV Bahia (ação câncer de mama), rádio interviews, Outubro Rosa palestra, parceria Restaurante Rancho Catarinense, capacitação COVID-19 (Ministério da Saúde, 2020).
- Absent, do not fabricate: prices, plan contents, before/after photos (CFN ethics).

## Product Principles

1. Every action ends in WhatsApp.
2. Only her real words, real photos and real reviews.
3. Prazer na alimentação, not restriction: the page should feel generous, not clinical.
